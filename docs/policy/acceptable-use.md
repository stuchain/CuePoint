# Acceptable use, export control and trademarks

What CuePoint may be used for, how it may be distributed across borders, and how its name and logo may be used.

## Acceptable Use Policy
### Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of CuePoint and its associated services.

### Acceptable Use

You may use CuePoint to:

- Enrich your Rekordbox playlists with Beatport metadata
- Process your own music library data locally
- Run the application for personal or commercial use within the scope of its intended functionality

### Prohibited Use

You must not:

1. **Abuse External Services**: Do not use CuePoint to make excessive requests to Beatport, DuckDuckGo, or other third-party services that could violate their terms or cause harm.
2. **Automate at Scale**: Do not run automated bulk processing or scraping that could overload or degrade third-party services.
3. **Circumvent Restrictions**: Do not use CuePoint to bypass rate limits, access controls, or terms of service of external services.
4. **Malicious Use**: Do not use CuePoint to distribute malware, violate privacy, or engage in illegal activities.

### Compliance

If you use CuePoint in a way that violates this policy or the terms of third-party services, we may discontinue support or access. We reserve the right to update this policy as needed.

### Contact

For questions about acceptable use, please open an issue in the CuePoint repository.

## Export Control
### Overview

CuePoint is open-source software. This document describes our distribution policy and export control considerations for international distribution.

### Distribution

- CuePoint is distributed publicly via GitHub and release assets.
- No intentional restrictions are placed on distribution by geography.
- Users are responsible for compliance with their local laws.

### Export Control Compliance

- CuePoint does not contain encryption or controlled technology beyond standard open-source libraries.
- Dependencies are common open-source packages (for example requests, Playwright, and Electron-side tooling) with permissive licenses.
- If you are in a jurisdiction that restricts software import/export, please verify compliance with your local regulations before downloading or using CuePoint.

### Embargoed Regions

We do not actively block access from any region. However, users in embargoed or restricted regions may be subject to GitHub's or other service providers' terms of service. Please ensure your use complies with applicable law.

### Contact

For questions about export control or distribution, open an issue in the CuePoint repository.

## Trademark and Brand Usage
### Overview

This document provides guidance on using the CuePoint name, logo, and brand assets.

### Permitted Use

You may use the CuePoint name and logo when:

- **Referring to the software**: Describing or linking to the CuePoint application.
- **Including in documentation**: Tutorials, guides, or documentation that reference CuePoint.
- **Contributing**: As part of contributions to the CuePoint project.

### Restrictions

- **Do not imply endorsement**: Do not use the CuePoint name or logo in a way that suggests official endorsement unless you have permission.
- **Do not modify**: Do not alter the logo (colors, proportions, elements) without permission.
- **Do not use for commercial branding**: Do not use CuePoint branding for unrelated products or services in a way that could cause confusion.

### Brand Assets

- **Logo**: Located in `docs/images/logo.png`
- **Usage**: Use the official logo when linking to or referencing CuePoint.

### Contact

For questions about trademark or brand usage, open an issue in the CuePoint repository.
